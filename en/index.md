## It lives within us.

From grandparents to parents. From parents to children. The same fire, for 170 years.

The anticipation, hands working together, familiar faces meeting again. In Luogosano, the days around the Feast of the Immaculate Conception preserve a tradition that spans generations and holds the community together.

The sacred and the everyday meet in the same place, year after year. The Fuochi Allavorati are an age-old ritual of sharing, belonging and community.

“A tradition is not staged. It is inherited.”

### Sunday 6 December

- **14:00 — La tirata re lo cippo**. Organised by Pro Loco Locossano APS

- **20:30 — Lighting of the Christmas tree**. The village gathers as the anticipation turns to light.

- **21:00 — I Bottari della Cantica Popolare**. In concert · Followed by a DJ set

✳ Food and wine stalls, with produce for sale from local farms.

### Monday 7 December

- **EVENING — The night of the bonfires**. Bonfires burn throughout the evening in the village neighbourhoods. Walk through the village and discover them, one by one.

- **ROAMING — Music meets the fire**. Two folk music groups roam between the bonfires.

### Tuesday 8 December

### What is on the programme?

6 December: tirata re lo cippo, Christmas tree lighting, a concert, a DJ set, and food and wine stalls. 7 December: neighbourhood bonfires and roaming musicians. 8 December: a photography contest and live music at 18:30 in Piazza A. De Gasperi.
